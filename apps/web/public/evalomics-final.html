Why the arithmetic matters

A number you can recompute is worth more than a number you can only believe.

Exact rational arithmetic

Derived values stay reduced integer numerator/denominator pairs. No floats, no drift. Rounding happens once, at display, half-to-even, to at most 12 places.

Evidence, not opinion

A calculation records its formula and inputs. It never assigns a recommendation and it never promotes itself to Verified — only a held post-rollout delta can do that.

Coverage you can audit

Money arrives as canonical decimal strings — JSON numbers are rejected. Missing counts stay null, never zero. A 30-day projection needs at least seven covered days.

Questions

What teams ask before connecting.

Do you change our traffic?↓

No. The connection is read-only. An experiment only starts after you approve the split, the slice size and the guardrail, and any run aborts itself if quality drops past the floor you set.

What counts as verified?↓

A change that has been rolled out and whose delta has held in observed spend against the pre-change counterfactual baseline for a full verification window. Interim test results are labelled Tested, never savings.

Where does the data live?↓

Usage records and traces stay in your tenant with per-workspace authorization. Enterprise workspaces can export the same lineage to Snowflake or BigQuery and recompute every figure themselves.

Why not charge a percentage of savings?↓

Because it would pay us to inflate estimates, and estimates are the part of this product that should be hardest to inflate. We charge on spend under observation instead.

Which providers are supported?↓

Direct provider billing exports plus gateway logs for OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible endpoints. Adapters are additive: an unsupported provider shows as uncovered spend rather than being silently estimated.
