# Proovance Brand System

Status: working brand pending final legal/trademark review before paid registration.

## Brand

**Proovance**

Pronunciation: **PROO-vance**

Name idea: **prove + advance**. It also intentionally evokes evidence provenance without using a generic cost-tool name.

Category: **AI Efficiency Intelligence**

Core promise:

> Find AI waste. Test safer fixes. Prove what actually improved.

Primary product sentence:

> Proovance helps AI teams find inefficient spend and work, test bounded alternatives against explicit quality constraints, and verify post-change impact.

## Product architecture

- **Proovance Work MRI** — evidence-backed diagnosis
- **Optimization Lab** — bounded candidate testing
- **Verified Impact** — post-change measurement
- **Proovance Research** — public benchmarks, methods, and AI Efficiency Index
- **Proovance Tools** — free calculators and acquisition utilities

## Taglines

Primary: **Find AI waste. Test safer fixes. Prove what actually improved.**

Short: **Analyze. Optimize. Prove.**

Search-oriented: **AI cost optimization, workflow efficiency, and verified impact.**

## Visual direction

Precise, high-trust, technical, premium, and fast.

Avoid generic AI brains, robot heads, neural-network stock imagery, crypto styling, exaggerated futuristic chrome, or gambling/finance hype.

Suggested palette:
- Midnight: #08111F
- Deep navy: #0F1B2D
- Signal cyan: #12C7E8
- Proof green: #38D996
- Electric blue: #3C7BFF
- Soft white: #F7FAFC
- Muted slate: #93A4B8

Typography:
- headings: Inter / Geist / similar geometric sans
- body: Inter / Geist / system sans
- data: JetBrains Mono / ui-monospace

## Logo direction

A simple geometric **P** built from:
1. an open diagnostic ring,
2. a forward optimization path,
3. a proof/check convergence point.

It must remain recognizable at favicon size and work in one color.

Do not reuse or imitate the discarded OptiProof mark.

## Motion identity

Two-second ident:
- 0.00–0.30s: one evidence point appears
- 0.30–0.70s: points resolve into a diagnostic ring
- 0.70–1.10s: ring opens into a forward path
- 1.10–1.45s: path locks into the Proovance P mark
- 1.45–1.75s: wordmark appears
- 1.75–2.00s: **Analyze. Optimize. Prove.**

Motion should feel engineered, not decorative.

## Voice

Clear, calm, evidence-led, useful, technical without being academic.

Prefer:
- "The evidence supports..."
- "Potential saving..."
- "This benchmark passed the declared quality floor."
- "We cannot verify this claim from the current evidence."

Avoid:
- "revolutionary"
- "guaranteed savings"
- "world's best"
- "magic"
- fabricated urgency or fear

## Account rollout rule

Before a paid domain purchase or irreversible account naming:
1. verify exact domain availability at a registrar,
2. run a final trademark screen in target launch jurisdictions,
3. verify preferred handles on each platform,
4. use the same avatar/name/bio/URL wherever possible.

No domain or handle is considered available merely because a search engine returned no result.
