## Step 1 — final name/domain/handle verification

The working brand is **Proovance**.

Before spending money or making irreversible registrations:

1. Run a current trademark collision screen in the United States, European Union, United Kingdom, and India for software/SaaS/AI/FinOps-related classes.
2. Check exact registrar availability for:
   - proovance.com
   - proovance.ai
   - proovance.io
   - proovance.dev
3. Check live signup availability for:
   - @proovance
   - @proovanceai
4. Check company/page naming on LinkedIn and Product Hunt.

Search-engine absence is not availability proof.

If a material software/trademark collision is found, STOP before registrations and report it.

If a paid domain is required, STOP and obtain explicit approval with exact registrar, annual price, renewal price, privacy/WHOIS option, and preferred domain.

## Targeted outbound

Do not mass spam.

Target teams where meaningful LLM usage is publicly plausible:

- AI-native SaaS
- AI agents
- AI support platforms
- AI content/workflow products
- agencies operating AI workloads
- software companies with visible OpenAI/Anthropic/Gemini infrastructure

For each target:

1. research current product
2. identify legitimate evidence of AI usage
3. identify role: founder/CTO/FinOps/AI platform lead
4. create a relevant useful artifact or observation
5. send one concise evidence-led message
6. follow up reasonably
7. stop if asked

Never fabricate a defect, spend estimate, loss, urgency, or security issue.

Core outbound angle:

> Most AI-cost tools tell you where the money went. Proovance is designed around the next question: what can you safely change without dropping below the required quality?
